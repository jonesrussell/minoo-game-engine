# Content provenance and release approval

Each vocabulary entry records a stable ID, exact spelling, English meaning, dialect or variant, source reference, attribution, permitted use and approval status. Record reviewer and review date when approved. Audio and artwork have independent source, rights and approval records.

Use clearly identified synthetic English fixtures or IDs for engineering until approved language material is available. Never fabricate translations or pronunciation. Missing audio means no playback control. Do not strip diacritics or silently merge language variants.

The public Minoo site is a reference for product behavior, not blanket permission to copy its dictionary, recordings or artwork. New source-code licensing must not imply ownership or relicensing of contributed content. Release checks must reject unresolved fixtures and missing required provenance.

Human review assesses learning quality and cultural presentation. Automated validation assesses structure, references and completeness of metadata. The initial backlog includes explicit licensing and content approval decisions.
